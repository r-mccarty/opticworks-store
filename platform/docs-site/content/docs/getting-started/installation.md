## Choosing a Location

For optimal performance, place your sensor:

- **1-2 meters** from the monitoring area
- **Elevated** position (1-1.5m from floor)
- **Clear line of sight** to the target area
- **Away from** heat sources and direct sunlight

## Mounting Options

### Wall Mount

1. Use the included screws or adhesive pad
2. Position the sensor at a 15-30° downward angle
3. Ensure the USB-C port is accessible

### Tabletop Placement

1. Place on a stable, flat surface
2. Use the rubber feet to prevent sliding
3. Angle toward the monitoring area

## Power Connection

1. Connect the USB-C cable to the sensor
2. Plug the power adapter into a wall outlet
3. The LED will flash blue during startup

{{< hint warning >}}
Do not use extension cords or power strips that may cause voltage fluctuations.
{{< /hint >}}
